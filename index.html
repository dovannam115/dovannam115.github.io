About

A data person with an actuary's eye for the numbers.

I'm Nam, a Data Analyst at PVI Digital. My work sits where insurance domain knowledge meets data: I take raw policy, premium and claims data and turn it into dashboards and pipelines that the business actually uses to price and steer the book.

Day to day that means designing Power BI reports, automating refreshes from SQL Server into Microsoft Fabric, and writing PySpark to crunch daily actuarial metrics, loss ratios, renewal rates, exposure and combined ratios, at scale.

I care about clarity: a dashboard should answer the question in one glance, and a pipeline should be reliable enough that nobody thinks about it.
